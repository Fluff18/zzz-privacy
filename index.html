Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

🌱 What zzz does

zzz is a personal goal-tracking app. You set quests (goals), check them off, build streaks, write short daily gratitude notes, and earn points toward rewards you pick for yourself. You can optionally add friends using a share code to see each other's progress.

📋 Information we collect

When you use zzz, we collect the information you give us directly:

  • Profile info — the display name and avatar emoji you choose, and your email address if you choose Sign in with Apple (Apple lets you hide your real email).
  • App content — the quests you create, your check-in history, streaks, points balance, reward choices, and redemption history.
  • Gratitude entries — the short reflections you write. These are private to you unless you choose to share them.
  • Friend connections — if you add a friend by share code, your display name, avatar, and streak become visible to that friend, and theirs to you.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising, and zzz does not contain third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

☁️ How we store and use your data

Your data is stored locally on your device and synced to our backend (hosted on Supabase) so it's available across sessions and devices, and so friend features work. We use this data only to operate the app's features described above — we do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

🔑 Third parties

We use the following service providers to operate zzz:

  • Supabase — database hosting for your profile and app data.
  • Sign in with Apple — if you choose to sign in, Apple authenticates your identity; we only receive the name and email (or Apple private relay email) you choose to share.

🗑️ Deleting your data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This immediately and permanently removes your profile, quests, gratitude entries, and friend connections from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

You can also email us at rahulkoushik1999@gmail.com to request deletion.

🧒 Children's privacy

zzz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

🔒 Security

We use industry-standard practices, including row-level security on our database, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at rahulkoushik1999@gmail.com.
