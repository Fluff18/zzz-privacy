❓ Frequently asked

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your quests, streaks, points, and gratitude entries.

How do I add a friend?
Go to the Buddies tab, share your code with a friend, and enter theirs to connect.

My data isn't syncing.
Make sure you have an internet connection and the app is up to date. If the issue persists, email us below.
